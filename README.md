# Warning Label for Humans: One Line, Big Laugh

Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/c/u-0xb3e689-warning-label-for-humans-one-line-big-laugh-40e31f85): the funniest single-line safety warning label that should come printed on every human being, 160 characters or fewer.

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> WARNING: Human may suddenly explain a dream in full. There is no off switch. Nodding is the only approved response.

115 characters, one line. The line was picked by a blind tournament: 10 candidates, two independent blind judges running full 45-pair round-robins against the published judge brief, then a head-to-head tiebreak in both orders. The winner won the tiebreak 2–0 on the brief's third criterion — it is the most universal human truth in the set, told in authentic label language.
